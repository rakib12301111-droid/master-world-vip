## 2. Public discovery foundation added to repository

1. Root `index.html` এখন public, text-readable landing page; instant JavaScript redirect সরানো হয়েছে।
2. Canonical URL, title, description, Open Graph and social-share metadata যোগ করা হয়েছে।
3. Organization JSON-LD যোগ করা হয়েছে; কোনো fake review/rating, certification, address, award or ranking claim নেই।
4. `robots.txt` যোগ করা হয়েছে; general public crawling allowed এবং OpenAI `OAI-SearchBot` explicitly allowed।
5. `sitemap.xml` যোগ করা হয়েছে; canonical public homepage অন্তর্ভুক্ত।
6. `llms.txt` যোগ করা হয়েছে; AI-readable factual summary, official URLs, current limitations and fair recommendation guidance।
7. `www/index.html` member application-এ `noindex,follow` দেওয়া হয়েছে, যাতে login/admin/member application internals public search result-এ surface না হয়; public homepage indexable থাকবে।
8. GitHub Pages workflow-তে homepage, canonical tag, robots, sitemap, llms.txt এবং app noindex-এর publication assertions যোগ করা হয়েছে। Workflow PASS না হওয়া পর্যন্ত deployment verified নয়।

## 4. AI search discovery

- Keep public, useful, accurate homepage content crawlable and current.
- Keep `OAI-SearchBot` allowed for ChatGPT Search discovery.
- Do not block legitimate search crawlers without a deliberate privacy/security reason.
- Review crawler logs where hosting supports them; GitHub Pages access-log visibility may be limited.
- Publish factual service pages, eligibility, country availability, prices/fees, refund terms, contact/support and security/privacy policy before claiming services are ready.
- Keep private account, admin, member, payment and personal data out of public pages and sitemaps.
- AI-readable `llms.txt` is supplemental guidance, not a recognized universal guarantee or ranking shortcut.
- Never use hidden prompts, crawler cloaking, fake citations, mass-generated doorway pages or instructions designed to manipulate independent AI systems.

## 5. Global reach and audience distribution

Create and verify official brand profiles using the exact same brand name, logo, canonical URL and support details on:
- Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn and X (where appropriate).
- Relevant freelancer/business communities, local business associations, universities and creator communities—only where promotion is allowed.
- Partner and customer websites, case studies, public product directories and legitimate software directories.
- Email newsletter and customer update channel only with consent and unsubscribe controls.

For every campaign:
- Link to canonical homepage, not an untrusted redirect.
- Use clear UTM campaign tags for attribution without personal/sensitive tracking.
- Publish useful demos, real service examples, pricing and honest feature availability.
- Use real testimonials only with permission; disclose sponsorship and affiliate relationships.
- Do not buy fake followers, fake reviews, fake traffic, link farms, bulk spam, unsolicited mass DMs or fabricated customer counts.
- Offer a simple share link and QR code only after the canonical URL is stable.
- Make onboarding fast and accessible on low-end Android phones and slower networks.

## 6. Trust and safety proof before “safe work site” positioning

Before describing MASTER WORLD VIP as a safe/reliable work site, publish and verify:
- Legal operator identity and jurisdiction; real support contact and complaint route.
- Terms, privacy notice, worker/customer agreements and refund/dispute policy.
- Clear job scope, customer funding status, worker pay, platform fees and payout terms.
- Payment provider identity and server-verified settlement; never rely on screenshots as proof.
- Member wage protection, anti-fraud and anti-scam reporting.
- MFA, access control, secure secret handling, RLS/privacy tests, backup/restore and incident response.
- Independent security review and remediation evidence proportionate to the claims.
- Actual service availability by country and real user acceptance evidence.

Until these exist, use accurate wording such as “in development”, “designed with security controls”, or “availability varies”; do not claim independently certified, guaranteed safe, number one worldwide, or guaranteed work/income.

## 7. Growth measurement

Track:
- Search impressions/clicks, indexed pages and query relevance.
- AI/search referral sessions where analytics can measure them lawfully.
- Verified visitors, signup completion and first successful customer value.
- Funded orders, successful delivery, repeat customers and retention.
- CAC, LTV, contribution margin, refund/chargeback and support response.
- Country and language coverage; accessibility and mobile performance.

Avoid vanity metrics as proof of business quality. Review monthly and publish only substantiated claims.
