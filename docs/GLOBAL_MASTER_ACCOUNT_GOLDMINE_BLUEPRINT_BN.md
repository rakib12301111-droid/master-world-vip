## 1. Vision and positioning

**MASTER WORLD VIP = One Global Master Account for the World.**

একটি account দিয়ে ব্যক্তি, creator, freelancer, customer, merchant, developer, agency এবং enterprise ব্যবহারকারী একই ecosystem-এ কাজ করবে। Account এক হলেও permission, data, wallet, tax, business workspace এবং financial liability আলাদা থাকবে।

“Goldmine / সোনার খনি” হলো দীর্ঘমেয়াদি ব্যবসায়িক লক্ষ্য—নিশ্চিত লাভ, নিশ্চিত গ্রাহক, নিশ্চিত কাজ বা নিশ্চিত সদস্য-আয় নয়। বাস্তব লাভ হবে তখনই যখন verified customer demand, delivered value, collected payment, positive unit economics এবং retained customers একসঙ্গে থাকবে।

## 2. Global Master Account foundation

1. One account / secure identity: email/phone verification, passkeys or MFA, recovery, device/session management, suspicious-login alerts.
2. Global profile: preferred language, country, timezone, verified contact, accessibility and notification preferences.
3. Multi-workspace: Personal, Customer, Creator, Freelancer/Worker, Merchant, Developer, Agency and Enterprise workspaces.
4. Role-based permissions: member, customer, seller, worker, reviewer, support, finance, country operator, admin and owner; least privilege and audit trail.
5. Country-aware experience: language, currency display, local tax, consumer rights, privacy, payment methods and legal restrictions.
6. One unified dashboard, inbox, support centre, service catalogue, order history and account security centre.
7. Data portability and privacy: consent, export/correction/deletion workflows, retention limits and no cross-workspace data leakage.
8. No forced social/contact upload; no sale of personal data; no sensitive targeting without lawful basis and consent.

## 3. Revenue engine portfolio

Each engine must have an owner, customer segment, product definition, pricing, acquisition channel, direct costs, support burden, fraud controls, refund rules, tax treatment and measurable unit economics. Engines remain disabled until those gates pass.

### A. Recurring software and membership revenue
- Free plan as a genuine entry tier with clear limits.
- Optional Pro, Creator, Business, Agency and Enterprise subscriptions.
- Monthly/annual billing, transparent renewal/cancellation, invoices, grace periods and refund handling.
- Paid features: higher quotas, collaboration, automation, analytics, integrations, team seats and priority support.
- Never silently upgrade, auto-charge without consent, or promise income merely for purchasing a plan.

### B. Global service and project marketplace
- Real customer orders for typing, translation, design, video editing, web/app development, data processing, research, customer support, bookkeeping assistance and other lawful deliverables.
- Verified clients, funded orders, clear scope, delivery criteria, revision limits, dispute process and acceptance evidence.
- Platform commission only from disclosed, eligible distributable net revenue.
- Member pay disclosed before acceptance and protected after acceptance.
- High-value USD offers are price targets only; quote based on scope, market demand, quality, delivery cost and customer funding.

### C. AI tools and usage-based services
- AI writing, translation, document processing, coding assistance, image generation where licensed, voice/transcription, research and workflow automation.
- Free preview quotas; transparent pay-per-use or subscription; usage meter and spend caps.
- Provider cost, abuse limits, safety review, privacy and licensed inputs/outputs considered.
- No AI job starts with a charge until user sees scope/price and consents.

### D. Business SaaS and workflow automation
- CRM, booking, inventory, invoicing, customer support inbox, appointment reminders, team task management, analytics and automated reporting.
- Monthly software fees, seat-based plans, setup/integration fees and support contracts.
- Business customer data isolated by tenant; backup, access controls, audit logs and service-level disclosure.

### E. Enterprise, white-label and API licensing
- Enterprise contracts, private workspaces, API access, usage tiers, integration fees, white-label licensing and managed deployments.
- Contract, security review, uptime/incident commitments and support capacity before sale.
- API keys stored securely server-side; quotas, rate limits, metering and revocation.

### F. Digital products and learning
- Templates, design assets, business documents, courses, guides, paid workshops and downloadable tools.
- Creator/seller consent, ownership/licensing checks, moderation, refund and consumer protection.
- Marketplace seller share and platform commission shown clearly.

### G. Creator and media services
- Creator subscriptions, paid communities where lawful, tips/donations, sponsored placements, licensed content and brand collaboration.
- Clear sponsorship disclosure, copyright moderation, child safety and payout verification.
- Never fabricate views, followers, reactions, clicks or ad engagement.

### H. Advertising and sponsorship
- Clearly labelled ads, sponsored listings and business sponsorship packages.
- Ad inventory sold only with valid consent/privacy controls and truthful audience measurement.
- No invalid traffic, click incentives, hidden ads or selling personal data.
- Ad revenue is recognized only after provider-confirmed settlement and invalid-traffic adjustments.

### I. Affiliate and referral partnerships
- Disclosed affiliate links and verified partner commissions for real purchases/services.
- No pyramid, pay-to-join, recruitment-based income or multi-level liability that is not fully funded and legally reviewed.
- Rewards only after partner settlement, refund window and fraud checks.
- Referral participation must remain free unless separately lawful and clearly disclosed; no member deposit to unlock earnings.

### J. Managed services and business operations
- Customer support, moderation, localization, data cleanup, catalog operations, QA/testing and back-office services under lawful customer contracts.
- Real customer demand, trained staff, measurable service quality and costed delivery.
- Automation assists; human review for high-impact or ambiguous decisions.

### K. Local commerce and booking
- Merchant directory, appointment/booking, service discovery, order routing and transaction facilitation.
- Revenue via disclosed merchant subscription, listing fee or commission after actual completed/settled transaction.
- Merchant verification, cancellation/refund handling and local licensing checks.

### L. Partnerships and distribution
- Telecom, education, software, payment, logistics and business-channel partnerships.
- Revenue share only under signed contract and settlement evidence.
- No claim of free global internet/connectivity until a licensed provider, capacity, hardware, funding and approvals exist.

## 4. Customer acquisition and retention flywheel

1. Start with a narrow Bangladesh-first segment where demand and payment acceptance can be proven.
2. Recruit verified business customers and obtain funded pilot orders before scaling worker capacity.
3. Publish real case studies, delivery samples, transparent pricing and customer reviews with consent.
4. Build organic search content, referral invitations, partner distribution and targeted advertising with truthful claims.
5. Improve onboarding, first-value time, repeat purchase, customer support and cancellation/refund experience.
6. Expand country-by-country only when local payment, tax, privacy, consumer protection and support are ready.
7. Track acquisition cost (CAC), activation, conversion, repeat purchase, churn, lifetime value (LTV), gross margin, contribution margin, refunds, chargebacks and support cost.
8. Do not purchase fake traffic or represent target customer counts as current customers.

## 5. The profit and cash-control system

Separate immutable ledgers and bank/provider settlement accounts for:
- Customer gross receipts and unsettled receivables.
- Taxes/withholding and payment/FX fees.
- Refunds, chargebacks and restricted customer funds.
- Member accepted wages and payable balances.
- Provider/compute/hosting and operating expenses.
- Company distributable net revenue.
- Admin/Owner approved USD entitlement and commission.
- USD-to-BDT conversion and actual BDT settlement.
- Referral/marketing budget and separately funded promotional prize pool.

Required controls:
- Provider-signed webhooks, idempotency, atomic posting, reconciliation and immutable audit log.
- Daily settlement matching and exception queue; no spendable balance for estimates or pending invoices.
- Gross revenue, net revenue, cash received, accounting profit and Owner withdrawal shown separately.
- Budget caps, reserve thresholds, fraud monitoring and emergency stop.
- No double counting, self-purchase income, fake orders, fake tasks, Ponzi/pyramid funding or member-wage diversion.

## 6. Admin/Owner USD and BDT operating model

- Attribute every eligible real revenue source to the approved Admin entitlement policy, including actual settled revenue associated with Free and Paid plan ecosystems.
- Free plan activation itself does not create money. Only real paid orders, subscriptions, ads, sponsorship or partner settlements can create revenue.
- Preserve the existing progressive commission request (20% lower-value / 35% higher-value) only on the approved distributable net base; do not invent the missing tier threshold.
- Record USD source ledger first; use a regulated provider's executable FX quote with timestamp, spread, fees, tax and expiry.
- Credit BDT only after the provider confirms actual conversion/settlement to the nominated verified account.
- If no approved provider integration or account authorization exists, show Pending/Manual—not automatic bank credit.
- Admin's own plan purchase is expense, not income. No artificial or guaranteed USD.

## 7. Member earning and fair-work protections

- Work only from real funded customer demand or authorized partner tasks.
- Member's exact BDT payable, USD equivalent, FX source/time, fee/tax, scope and acceptance criteria shown before consent.
- Once accepted, BDT payable cannot be reduced because FX moved.
- Server-side workers may run while a member is offline; member phone is never secretly used as a worker.
- No mandatory deposit, task-unlock fee or paid plan to access legitimate work.
- No guarantee of daily jobs or income. Fair assignment, skill/quality matching, workload limits and dispute appeal.
- Member Auto Work remains disabled until the existing refund-reserve, operating-fund, isolation, provider/legal/security and acceptance gates pass.

## 8. Promotional bonus and prize controls

Any random USD member bonus is a separately funded promotion, not income or investment return. No purchase necessary, no paid-plan advantage, no guaranteed winner/timing. Legal review/permits, published rules, audited randomness, tax and payout provider must pass before enabling. If any gate is missing, OFF.

## 9. Global legal, trust and safety gates

- Country-by-country legal assessment before offering financial, payment, escrow, lending, investment, gambling, telecom or regulated services.
- Do not operate as a bank, wallet custodian, remittance provider or payment institution without required authorization and regulated partners.
- KYC/AML/sanctions and fraud controls where legally required; privacy, data protection, consumer rights, tax, labor and IP compliance.
- No investment deposits, guaranteed returns, pyramid schemes, deceptive income claims, unauthorized scraping, fake engagement or unlawful data access.
- Security: MFA, least privilege, RLS, encryption, secret management, backups, incident response, penetration testing and recovery drills.
- Accessibility, multilingual support, clear terms, transparent fees, complaint handling and human escalation.

## 10. Rollout roadmap and launch gates

### Phase 0 — Foundation
- Master Account identity, workspace/roles, security, consent, audit log, privacy and finance data model.
- Product catalogue and service definitions; no money movement yet.
- Verified country, legal and provider readiness matrix.

### Phase 1 — Bangladesh pilot
- Select a small number of high-demand services; sign real customers; run funded pilot orders.
- Payment collection, refunds, member wage protection and manual finance reconciliation.
- Measure contribution margin and customer repeat rate before expansion.

### Phase 2 — Repeatable revenue
- Launch selected subscriptions, AI usage billing, marketplace commissions and business SaaS.
- Add provider-confirmed payouts and Admin USD/BDT conversion only after partner approval.
- Customer support, fraud controls and operational monitoring.

### Phase 3 — International expansion
- Add country/currency/payment/tax/legal adapters one country at a time.
- Localize product, support, privacy and payout methods.
- Enterprise/API/white-label only after security and support capacity.

### Phase 4 — Scale
- Expand genuine catalogue towards 2,000+ categories only when each has demand, a customer, contract/permission, pricing, delivery capacity and positive unit economics.
- Scale customers and concurrent jobs independently; no fake or guaranteed 10-million customer claim.
- Add automation, redundancy and capacity only against funded demand and measured margins.

**Launch rule:** A module is not LIVE because it is listed in a plan or UI. It needs code, configuration, provider/legal readiness, security review, test evidence and real-world acceptance.

## 11. Management dashboard — metrics that define a real business

- Revenue: gross billings, collected cash, net settled revenue, recurring revenue, revenue by engine/country/currency.
- Profit: gross margin, contribution margin, operating expenses, refunds, chargebacks and cash runway.
- Customers: verified active customers, first purchase, repeat rate, churn, CAC and LTV.
- Marketplace: funded orders, fill rate, on-time delivery, acceptance, dispute rate, member utilization and protected payable.
- Payments: settlement lag, reconciliation exceptions, FX slippage, payout success and failed transfers.
- Trust: fraud loss, security incidents, complaints, privacy requests and support response.
- Admin: approved entitlement, USD available, USD converted, actual BDT credited and pending reconciliation.
