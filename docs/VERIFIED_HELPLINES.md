# Verified emergency helpline directory

- Dataset: www/data/helplines-BD.json
- Every record carries source name, official source URL and verification date.
- The current Bangladesh dataset is a manually curated snapshot based on official Bangladesh Police, National Portal, Department of Disaster Management AWARE, and DESCO pages checked 2026-11-20.
- DESCO central hotline 16120 and Turag service centre 01324-435978 are sourced from DESCO's official service list.
- The app fetches the JSON when online and stores the last successfully fetched copy locally for offline viewing. The static site itself does not automatically monitor government pages or guarantee instant change detection. Updating the repository dataset is required when a provider changes its number.
- Non-Bangladesh countries are intentionally left without guessed numbers until official sources have been verified.
- In an emergency, call the national emergency number where available and follow official authorities. A listed number may change; always use source metadata and update date.
