# Verified emergency helpline directory

The app stores official-source records in www/data/helplines-BD.json. Each entry has source name, official URL, and verification date.

Bangladesh records are based on official Bangladesh Police, Bangladesh National Portal, Department of Disaster Management AWARE, Power Division, and DESCO pages checked 2026-11-20. DESCO central hotline 16120 and Turag service centre 01324-435978 are listed by DESCO.

The app fetches the JSON when online and stores the last successfully fetched copy locally for offline viewing. The static website does not independently monitor government websites; a provider changing a number requires a dataset update and redeployment. Do not promise instant updates until an authoritative feed or monitored backend is built.

Other countries are not populated with guessed numbers. Add a country's contacts only after verifying official government or provider sources.

In an emergency, use the official national emergency number and follow authorities. The cached directory may become outdated.
